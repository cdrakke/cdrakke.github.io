# Design System: Drekyz's minihompy

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Minihompy Room"**

The site is a room you visit, not a hero you scroll past. Everything sits on one committed blush field printed with a tiny diamond motif; a cream paper sheet floats on it inside a plum line, and every panel, control, chip, and label on that sheet is a sticker: an outlined pastel shape with a hard 2-4px lift underneath. The centre of the home page is an isometric SVG room drawn in the same hand (plum outlines, pastel fills, soft drop shadows), and its furnishings are the site's links. Density is high and friendly: three columns of small type, many stickers, few empty areas. The look is a single light scheme; there is no dark mode and no dark hero.

The build refuses the dark hero, the skill cloud, and the card grid. Depth is physical rather than atmospheric: offset lifts, not diffuse glows; a real press on every control (up 1px on hover, down 2px on active). Motion is one authored moment, the "settle", in which the room and panels land from 10px above with a 70ms stagger, and after that the only things that move are the record when music plays and the object you are pointing at.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Blush ground, paper sheet, sticker whites: the palette is committed at page scale, not accents on grey.
- Four pastels (sky, butter, mint, blossom) are role colours for stickers; plum ink is the only text colour.
- Plum line on everything: 2px on panels and controls, 1.6px on chips and inner surfaces.
- Hard offset lifts (0 3px 0) are the signature shadow; blur appears only on the sheet and the floating tab bar.
- Fredoka display on Nunito body, both heavy; tabular numerals everywhere.
- One settle entrance, one spinning record, reduced-motion turns all of it off.

## Colors

The palette is a pastel sticker sheet on a blush ground: warm cream surfaces, four candy role colours, and one plum ink for every line and every letter.

### Primary
- **Blush** (`blush`): the page field behind everything, the ground of the diamond motif, the `bgm-player` body, the teaser ledger, and the resting nav tab hover. It is the world's colour, not an accent.
- **Paper** (`paper`): the floating sheet that holds every page, faint-ruled with 22px lines at 2.8% ink.
- **Sticker White** (`white`): control and chip default fill, icon `currentColor`, the lightbox frame.
- **Veils** (`veil-soft`, `veil`, `veil-strong`): translucent paper at 55 / 72 / 86%. Veil-soft is the room panel; veil is every other panel and the count pill inside a button; veil-strong is the floating tab bar and diary count pills.

### Secondary
- **Sky** (`sky`): panel-title default tone, "Leave a note" action, location chip, and the room's striped walls. Sky-deep is the input focus ring.
- **Butter** (`butter`): the site ribbon, the resume action, the ledger Send, the Diary panel tone, volume dots when lit, text selection, and the underline colour of every inline link (butter-deep).
- **Mint** (`mint`): the active nav tab, the primary "Visit my work" action, the availability chip, and the Room panel tone.
- **Blossom** (`blossom`): the Ledger tone and warning chips; blossom-deep is the caret colour and the room's "PASSED" stamp.

### Tertiary
- **Hover Tints** (`mint-hover`, `sky-hover`, `butter-hover`): each sticker tone one step lighter under the pointer, on sticker buttons only.
- **Reply Inks** (`ok-ink`, `bad-ink`): the ledger status line after a send; the only two coloured text values in the world.
- **Gold Ink** (`gold-ink`): the "AWARDED" stamp text and its rule on the shelf.
- **Wood** (`wood`): room furniture only (desk, shelves, frame), drawn as the `WOOD` face palette (`#E2C4A2` top, wood left, `#B88E6B` right).
- **Success** (`success`): the 8px status dot on "Open to remote" and "live" chips, and the server-rack lamp glow in the room.

### Neutral
- **Plum Ink** (`ink`): the only text colour, focus rings, scrollbar thumb (ink-2), the stroke of every icon and every line in the room.
- **Ink 2** (`ink-2`): scrollbar thumb; the room's `PLUM` top face.
- **Ink Soft** (`ink-soft`): secondary text (subtitles, placeholders, uppercase field labels, aside text).
- **Line** (`line`): every border, at 2px (`--border`) or 1.6px (`--border-thin`).

### Named Rules
**The One Ink Rule.** Text, strokes, focus rings, and icon outlines are always plum ink or a plum alpha. Pastels colour surfaces, never letters. The three sanctioned exceptions are stamps and replies: ok-ink, bad-ink, gold-ink.

**The Sticker Tone Rule.** A pastel appears as the whole fill of an outlined sticker (panel title, chip, button, tab), never as a wash, gradient, or tint behind text on paper. Tones carry meaning by habit: mint for "here / go", butter for "take / send", sky for "write", blossom for "warn / ledger".

**The Paper Under Everything Rule.** Content lives on the paper sheet; the blush field and its diamond motif are visible only as the margin around it and under the floating tab bar.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Fredoka 400-700 (with Trebuchet MS, Verdana, sans-serif)
**Body Font:** Nunito 600-900, normal and italic (with Segoe UI, Helvetica Neue, Arial, sans-serif)

**Character:** Rounded and heavy on both sides. Fredoka does headings, panel titles, and every control label; Nunito does every sentence, chip, and counter at 700 or heavier. Nothing on the page is set lighter than 600. Body defaults to `tabular-nums` so counters and years line up.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (Fredoka 700, 40px, 1.1): the handle "Drekyz" in the profile panel; page titles on diary, shelf, and ledger use the same size at 600.
- **Headline** (Fredoka 600, 28px, 1.1): page titles on phones (≤760px) and the 404 title.
- **Title** (Fredoka 600, 22px, 1.1): section heads inside panels (profile subsections, project heads).
- **Control** (Fredoka 600, 15px, 1.2): sticker buttons, nav tabs, the ribbon (17px), the "BGM" title (500). Panel titles use the same face at 13px.
- **Body** (Nunito 700, 15px, 1.5): running text; long copy is capped at 68-70ch on diary entries.
- **Body Small** (Nunito 800, 13px, 1.25): chips, diary summaries, ledger hint text, most secondary copy.
- **Label** (Nunito 900, 11px, 0.3-0.5px tracking, uppercase, ink-soft): form field labels, group headings such as "FRIENDS", and the count pills inside buttons and tabs.
- **Micro** (Nunito 900, 10px, 0.4-0.5px tracking, uppercase): stat labels ("PROJECTS", "MOOD"), the "TODAY'S NOTE" tag, figure labels on the shelf and diary. Uppercase only; never for running text.

### Named Rules
**The Heavy Pair Rule.** Fredoka for anything you press or a title; Nunito for anything you read. Neither ever drops below weight 600.

**The Tabular Rule.** Numbers are always tabular so counters, years, and route counts align; never override `font-variant-numeric` on a stat.

## Layout

The whole site is one frame: a 22px blush margin (`--frame-pad`, 10px at ≤720px) around a centred paper sheet capped at 1396px, with a 62px floating tab bar pinned to the bottom (`--nav-height`) and a matching bottom padding so the sheet always reaches the bar. Four diamond corner stickers sit fixed in the viewport corners on desktop and are hidden at ≤720px. The topbar carries the butter ribbon on the left and status chips on the right; at ≤640px only the BGM chip survives.

Home is a three-column grid, `290px minmax(0,1fr) 322px` with a 14px gutter: profile left, room centre, diary and ledger right. At ≤1180px it becomes two columns (`270px 1fr`) with the room spanning full width; at ≤860px a single column. Inner pages use a single centred column (diary entries cap at 980px) with `auto-fit, minmax(280px, 1fr)` grids for screenshot sets and a `1.4fr 1fr` split for body-plus-sidebar.

**The Room on Phones Rule.** At ≤640px the room does not shrink: it keeps a 620px minimum width and scrolls sideways inside its panel with a 6% edge mask and a "swipe to look around" hint, and every object in it is repeated as a tappable legend chip below. Nothing in the room becomes unreachable on a phone.

Spacing is the 4px-based ramp `4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 28, 40`. Panels pad `20px 16px 16px`; the sheet pads `16px 20px 28px` (12px on phones). Component-internal gaps run 6-10px.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is sticker depth: every raised thing has a plum line and a hard, unblurred offset underneath it, as if it were cut from card and stuck down. Blur is reserved for two structural surfaces (the paper sheet and the floating tab bar) and for the portrait photo. Nothing glows.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Lift** (`box-shadow: 0 3px 0 rgba(58,46,63,.16)`): the signature. Sticker buttons, the ribbon, the active nav tab, the bgm player, hovered diary rows.
- **Lift Small** (`box-shadow: 0 2px 0 rgba(58,46,63,.10)`): chips, panel titles, small tiles, diary rows at rest.
- **Lift Large** (`box-shadow: 0 4px 0 rgba(58,46,63,.14)`): panels; the tab bar stacks it with Shadow Soft.
- **Shadow Soft** (`box-shadow: 0 8px 22px rgba(58,46,63,.14)`): the floating tab bar only, under its Lift Large.
- **Shadow Photo** (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 1.6px var(--line), 0 6px 14px rgba(58,46,63,.18)`): the portrait sticker and the OG-card portrait.
- **Sheet** (`0 0 0 6px rgba(255,253,248,.7), 0 10px 30px rgba(58,46,63,.14), inset 0 0 0 1px rgba(255,255,255,.8)`): the paper sheet alone.
- **Press** (hover `0 4px 0 rgba(58,46,63,.18)` + `translateY(-1px)`; active `0 1px 0 rgba(58,46,63,.18)` + `translateY(2px)`): the sticker button's real press.

### Named Rules
**The Hard Lift Rule.** A shadow on a control or panel is a solid offset with zero blur. Blur belongs only to the sheet, the tab bar, and photographs.

**The Real Press Rule.** Every button rises 1px on hover and sinks 2px on active, with the lift shrinking to match. A control that does not move is not a sticker.

## Shapes

Two silhouettes: pills and rounded cards. Anything you press, tag, or label is a full pill (`999px`): buttons, chips, panel titles, nav tabs, inputs, the ribbon, the player. Anything that holds content is a rounded rectangle on the 10/14/18/22 ramp, with two smaller steps for ornament: hairline (2px) on tape strips, the active-tab mark, the level bars, and the corner diamonds; tight (6px) on certificate frames and shelf mats. Then small tiles and the focus-ring corner at 10px, ledger boxes, textareas, and diary rows at 14px, panels at 18px, the sheet and the portrait at 22px. Play and mute are true circles.

Every shape carries a plum line: 2px (`--border`) on panels, buttons, the sheet, the nav, and the player; 1.6px (`--border-thin`) on chips, panel titles, inputs, and inner boxes; 1-1.4px on the smallest pills (count badges, dots). Panel titles hang over the panel's top edge (`top: -15px`) as a separate sticker. Diamonds (a 12px square rotated 45°, 2px radius) are the motif geometry, repeated in the field pattern and the four corner stickers.

The room follows the same hand in SVG: plum strokes at 1.2-1.6 with 0.55-0.7 opacity, pastel face fills (`WOOD`, `PLUM`, `CREAM`, `ROSE` palettes, top face lightest), and `tag()` sticker labels (white or pastel pill, ink stroke at .7, Nunito 800 at 9.5-10px).

## Components

### Buttons (StickerButton)
Every control is a rounded sticker with a real press.
- **Shape:** full pill (`999px`), 2px plum line, Fredoka 600 at 15px, icon at 18px on the left, optional count pill on the right.
- **Default:** sticker white fill, ink text, `9px 16px`, Lift shadow.
- **Tones:** mint (primary "go" action), sky (write / email), butter (take / send), blush, blossom. Mint, sky, and butter lighten to their `-hover` tints under the pointer.
- **Hover / Active:** rises 1px with a 4px lift; sinks 2px with a 1px lift; 160ms `--ease-out`.
- **Small:** `6px 12px`, 13px, icon at 15px.
- **Count pill:** Nunito 900 at 11px, veil fill, 1px line, full pill, `1px 7px`.

### Chips (StickerChip)
- **Style:** full pill, 1.6px line, white fill, Nunito 800 at 13px, `4px 11px`, Lift Small. Optional 8px status dot (success, butter, blossom-deep, ink) or 14px icon on the left.
- **Tones:** sky, butter, mint, blush, blossom as whole fills. `xs` size is `2px 8px` at 11px.
- **State:** link chips rise 1px on hover; there is no selected state.

### Cards / Containers (Panel)
- **Corner Style:** 18px.
- **Background:** veil over paper (the room panel uses veil-soft).
- **Shadow Strategy:** Lift Large.
- **Border:** 2px plum line.
- **Internal Padding:** `20px 16px 16px`, leaving room for the title sticker that hangs over the top edge at -15px: a 1.6px-lined pill in the panel's tone, Fredoka 600 at 13px with a 14px icon and an optional Nunito 900 aside in ink-soft.
- **Entrance:** panels take `.settle` with `--i` set for the stagger.

### Inputs / Fields (LedgerForm)
- **Style:** white fill, 1.6px line, full pill for text inputs and 14px radius for the textarea, Nunito 700 at 13px, `8px 14px`, an inset `0 2px 0 rgba(58,46,63,.06)` press line, ink-soft placeholder.
- **Label:** Nunito 900 at 11px, uppercase, 0.4px tracking, ink-soft, indented 12px to sit over the pill.
- **Focus:** 3px sky-deep ring at 1px offset (the one place the ring is not ink).
- **Status:** live region under the fields; success text in ok-ink, failure text in bad-ink. Submit button reads "Writing it in..." then "Signed".
- **Ledger box:** blush fill, 1.6px line, 14px radius. The home teaser is one pill input plus a butter Send that leads to /ledger.

### Screenshots and Lightbox (diary entry)
Screenshots sit in a justified flex row: each capture keeps its own aspect at a shared 250px height (180px on phones), in a 14px-radius white tile with a 1.6px line and Lift Small. Clicking opens a native `<dialog>`: the frame is a white sticker (2px line, 18px radius, `10px 10px 8px`, Shadow Soft) with the image at 10px radius and a Nunito 800 caption in ink-2, over a backdrop of ink at 72% with a 3px blur. Controls are 40px circles with the 2px line and Lift: butter close at the top-right corner, mint prev/next at the sides (hidden for a single shot). Escape closes; arrow keys step.

### Navigation (BottomNav)
- **Style:** fixed pill bar at the bottom, `min(100% - 2*frame-pad, 1396px)` wide, 62px tall, veil-strong with 6px backdrop blur, 2px line, Lift Large plus Shadow Soft, `6px 8px` padding. The `bgm-player` rides at its right end.
- **Tabs:** Fredoka 600 at 15px, 18px icon, transparent 2px border, `7px 16px`; hover fills blush; active fills mint, gains the line and Lift, and shows a 16x3px ink bar beneath. Count pills as in buttons.
- **Mobile:** 58px tall at ≤720px with 13px tabs and no counts; labels drop at ≤420px leaving icons only.

### Icons (Icon)
Authored for this world: a 16-unit grid, 1.4 stroke in plum ink (`#3A2E3F`), `currentColor` fills that default to sticker white and inherit the parent sticker's tone. Sizes 12-18px in practice. No icon font, no third-party glyph set.

### Record Player (bgm-player)
A blush pill (44px min height, 2px line, Lift) holding a 32px white play circle, five 4px level bars that animate 4-16px in a 900ms alternate loop while playing, the track title in Fredoka 500 with the artist in ink-soft below, five 12px volume dots that fill butter, and a 28px mute circle. Persists across page navigations; the topbar chip and the room's turntable mirror its state. Meta and volume hide at ≤720px.

### The Room (Room + draw.ts)
A 700x630 isometric SVG: striped sky walls, a checker floor in paper and `#F3E6EE`, wood furniture, a plum server rack and monitors, and a butter-ringed rug. Every visitable object is a link (or a button for the turntable) wrapped in `.obj` (the "Desk · 2 monitors" label belongs to the terminal monitor, which links to /diary), rising 3px on hover or focus-within with its `tag()` label brightening; keyboard focus thickens the label stroke to 2.4. Pieces land with the settle stagger, stage first. While music plays, `.vinyl` spins (2.4s linear) and `.notes` float; the rack lamp pulses on a 2.8s loop at all times. All loops and hover lifts stop under reduced motion.

### Motion
One ease for everything that settles: `cubic-bezier(.22,1,.36,1)` over 620ms, `translateY(-10px) scale(.985)` to rest, delayed `--i * 70ms`. State transitions use `cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1)` at 160ms (press, tint) or 260ms (object lift). `prefers-reduced-motion` collapses every animation and transition to 0.001ms and removes the object lift.

### Browser Surfaces
Selection is butter with ink text; carets are blossom-deep; `:focus-visible` is a 3px ink outline at 3px offset with a 10px corner; scrollbars are thin, ink-2 thumb on a blush track (10px, pill, 2px blush inset on WebKit). `color-scheme: light` is declared.

### Bare Surfaces
The print resume (`/resume`) and the social card (`/og`) render with `bare`: no ribbon, corners, tab bar, sheet border, or motif. The resume keeps the type pairing at print sizes with near-black text and a blue link colour; the OG card is a 1200x630 blush field with the motif, a 3px-lined paper card, the portrait with Shadow Photo, and the handle in Fredoka 700 at 54px.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** put content on the paper sheet and keep the blush field as the margin, with the diamond motif at 36px tiles.
- **Do** give every raised surface a plum line and a hard lift: 2px line + `0 3px 0` on controls, 2px + `0 4px 0` on panels, 1.6px + `0 2px 0` on chips.
- **Do** make every button press: up 1px on hover, down 2px on active.
- **Do** use a pastel as the whole fill of an outlined sticker, and pick the tone by habit (mint go, butter take, sky write, blossom warn).
- **Do** set titles and controls in Fredoka 600 and everything readable in Nunito 700-900 with tabular numerals.
- **Do** enter with one settle (620ms, `--ease-settle`, 70ms stagger via `--i`) and honour reduced motion.
- **Do** draw icons and room objects on the 16-grid in plum ink with `currentColor` fills.
- **Do** keep the room reachable on phones: sideways scroll at ≥620px width plus the legend chips.
- **Do** use the veils (55 / 72 / 86% white) for translucent paper and the hover tints for pastel hovers instead of new alpha or hex values.

### Don't:
- **Don't** add a dark mode or a dark hero; the world is one light scheme (`color-scheme: light`).
- **Don't** put pastel or coloured text on paper; text is plum ink or an ink alpha, with ok-ink, bad-ink, and gold-ink reserved for reply lines and stamps.
- **Don't** use blurred or ambient shadows on controls or panels; blur belongs to the sheet, the tab bar, and photos only.
- **Don't** replace the authored icon set with an icon font or a third-party glyph library.
- **Don't** lay projects out as a card grid or skills as a cloud; the diary list and the shelf are the forms.
- **Don't** add a second entrance animation or continuous motion beyond the record, the level bars, and the lamp.
- **Don't** set any text lighter than weight 600, or use the 10px micro size for anything but uppercase labels.
